# We have some bad news...

### 29/10/2026

First Off, Amaze, the parent company of Spring/Teespring, has shut down the service - including our main store - and are replacing it with "Amaze Commerce". We have decieded to *not* move over our store and instead focus on building our own store from the ground up with Shopify. This gives us more control over the products we sell as well as the ability to directly assist customers (thats you!) with order and shipping problems. This, however, comes with the downside that products will need to be significantly more expensive to cover the recurring costs of Shopify as well as the manual processes of developing the site, managing listings, signing manufacturing contracts, handling shipping, etc.

We have updated our policies to reflect the fact we no longer work with Amaze. That said, it will take us several months until we're able to launch the store, so we have not yet added details about that. you can read the new policies [here](https://transcendbinary.co.uk/legal/)
